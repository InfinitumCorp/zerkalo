# Seed brief (fictional, for demonstration)

- The city transport department has proposed a three-month weekend pilot closing the central
  boulevard ring to private cars from 10:00 to 23:00 on Saturdays and Sundays.
- Public transport, taxis for passengers with reduced mobility, and emergency vehicles keep access.
- Supporters point to similar pilots in other European capitals and to summer events programming.
- Critics worry about displaced traffic on the Garden Ring, parking, and deliveries to small shops.
- Early comments on city Telegram channels are split; drivers' groups are the most vocal opponents.
- The city says it will run a resident vote after the pilot.
